## BETWEEN

**__________________________**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at **__________________________** (hereinafter referred to as the **“Disclosing Party”**, which shall include its successors and permitted assigns)

AND

**__________________________**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at **__________________________** (hereinafter referred to as the **“Receiving Party”**, which shall include its successors and permitted assigns)

The Disclosing Party and Receiving Party shall collectively be referred to as the **“Parties.”**

---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to explore a potential **merger, acquisition, investment, joint venture, or similar strategic transaction** (the **“Proposed Transaction”**).

In connection with this evaluation, the Disclosing Party may share confidential business, financial, technical, and legal information.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

“Confidential Information” includes all non-public information disclosed in any form, including:

* Financial statements and projections
* Business plans and strategies
* Customer and vendor data
* Intellectual property and trade secrets
* Technical systems and processes
* Legal, operational, and compliance records
* Details of the Proposed Transaction

Confidential Information includes analyses, summaries, or derivatives prepared by the Receiving Party.

---

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

* Is publicly available without breach
* Was already lawfully known
* Is independently developed
* Is required to be disclosed under law or regulatory authority

---

## 4. Obligations of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

* Maintain strict confidentiality
* Use information solely for evaluating the Proposed Transaction
* Restrict access to authorized personnel and advisors
* Ensure advisors are bound by confidentiality obligations
* Not disclose information without written consent

---

## 5. Non-Solicitation

For a period of **2 years** from this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall not solicit employees, customers, or vendors of the Disclosing Party without written consent.

---

## 6. Non-Circumvention

The Receiving Party shall not bypass the Disclosing Party to directly engage with business contacts introduced during discussions.

---

## 7. Return or Destruction of Information

Upon request or termination of discussions, the Receiving Party shall:

* Return or destroy all Confidential Information
* Confirm destruction in writing

---

## 8. No Obligation to Proceed

This Agreement does not obligate either Party to complete any transaction.

---

## 9. No License Granted

No intellectual property rights or licenses are granted under this Agreement.

---

## 10. Term

This Agreement shall remain valid for **3 years** from execution.

Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for **5 years**.

---

## 11. Remedies

The Receiving Party acknowledges that breach may cause irreparable harm.

The Disclosing Party may seek:

* Injunctive relief
* Specific performance
* Monetary damages

---

## 12. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the **laws of India**.

---

## 13. Jurisdiction & Arbitration

Any disputes shall be:

* Subject to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996
* Seat and venue: **______________ (India)**
* Arbitration by a sole arbitrator mutually appointed

---

## 14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding and supersedes prior discussions.

---

## 15. Severability

Invalid provisions shall not affect remaining terms.

---

## 16. Amendments

Any changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
