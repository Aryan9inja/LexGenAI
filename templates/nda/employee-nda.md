# Employee Confidentiality Agreement

**Template for Indian Companies and Organisations**

---

This Agreement is effective when signed by and between **___________ Private Limited** (hereinafter referred to as the **“Company”**), a private limited company having its registered office at **____________**, and **________________**, (hereinafter referred to as the **“Employee”**), S/o. Mr. **______________**, residing at **_________________________**, collectively referred to as the **“Parties.”**

The Company has employed Mr./Ms. **___________________________** since/from **_____** and this agreement is intended to protect the confidential information disclosed by the Company in the course of employment to Mr./Ms. **_____________.**

In consideration of the remuneration paid by the Company, the receipt and adequacy of which is hereby acknowledged, the Employee agrees as follows:

---

## 1. Confidential Information

The Parties agree that information disclosed orally or in writing or made available by the Company to the Employee, including but not limited to:

* Information acquired from employees
* Trade secrets
* Strategic plans
* Invention plans and disclosures
* Customer information
* Computer programs and software codes
* Databases and suppliers
* Distribution channels
* Marketing studies
* Intellectual property
* Product and process information
* Designs, business plans, opportunities, and finances
* Research, development, and know-how
* Personnel information
* Confidential third-party information
* Technology-related information
* All other tangible or intangible materials

shall be deemed **“Confidential Information.”**

The existence and terms of this Agreement shall also be treated as Confidential Information.

Any Confidential Information disclosed prior to execution of this Agreement shall also be subject to its terms.

---

## 2. Restrictions and Exceptions

The Employee agrees to maintain confidentiality and prevent unauthorized use or dissemination of Confidential Information for a period of **two (2) years** from the date of last disclosure by the Company.

---

## 3. Non-Disclosure

The Employee agrees that he/she shall:

* Not use Confidential Information for personal gain
* Not provide products/services using Company Confidential Information
* Protect Confidential Information with reasonable care
* Limit internal disclosure only to those who need it for job responsibilities

---

## 4. Ownership of Confidential Information

All Confidential Information and materials delivered by the Company remain the property of the Company. No license or rights are granted to the Employee.

---

## 5. Return of Materials and Documents

Upon written request, the Employee shall return, erase, or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information, including:

* Computer programs
* Documentation
* Financial statements
* Notes and plans
* Drawings
* Customer information

The Employee must certify compliance upon request.

---

## 6. Non-Circumvention

The Employee shall not circumvent the Company by working directly with clients, associates, or vendors introduced by the Company.

This clause remains effective for **two (2) years** after termination.

---

## 7. Non-Solicitation

For **two (2) years** after termination, the Employee shall not solicit, provide services to, or assist others in working with Company clients.

---

## 8. No Tampering

During employment and for two (2) years after termination, the Employee shall not:

* Influence suppliers to stop business with the Company
* Influence customers to curtail business
* Influence employees to leave the Company

---

## 9. Remedy

Unauthorized disclosure shall be treated as fraud causing irreparable harm.

The Company may seek:

* Injunctive relief
* Monetary damages exceeding **₹10,00,000 per breach**
* Any other legal remedies

---

## 10. Termination

This Agreement shall remain effective until:

* Terminated in writing by both parties, OR
* Two (2) years from termination of employment

---

## 11. General Provisions

* This Agreement supersedes all prior agreements
* Amendments must be in writing and signed by all parties
* Waiver of one breach does not waive future breaches
* Invalid provisions shall not affect remaining provisions

---

## 12. Acknowledgement

The Employee acknowledges access to trade secrets, customers, confidential data, and goodwill, and agrees not to use or disclose such information.
