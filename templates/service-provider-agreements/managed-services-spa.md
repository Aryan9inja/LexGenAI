# Managed Services Agreement (MSA / SPA)

This Managed Services Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is made on this **___ day of ____**, 20****.

## BETWEEN

**[Client Name]**, having its registered office at **[Client Address]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Client"**, which shall include its successors and permitted assigns)

AND

**[Service Provider Name]**, having its principal place of business at **[Service Provider Address]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Service Provider"**, which shall include its successors and permitted assigns)

The Client and Service Provider shall collectively be referred to as the **"Parties."**

---

## 1. Scope of Managed Services

The Service Provider agrees to provide ongoing managed services including but not limited to:

* IT infrastructure monitoring and maintenance
* System administration and technical support
* Network and cybersecurity management
* Application management and support
* Helpdesk services

Detailed service scope and service levels shall be defined in **Annexure A / Service Level Agreement (SLA)**.

---

## 2. Term of Agreement

This Agreement shall commence on **[Start Date]** and remain valid for **[Duration – e.g., 12/24/36 months]**, unless terminated earlier as per this Agreement.

---

## 3. Service Levels & Performance Standards

The Service Provider shall:

* Maintain agreed uptime and service availability levels
* Respond to incidents within defined response times
* Provide periodic service performance reports
* Maintain qualified personnel and adequate resources

---

## 4. Fees & Payment Terms

The Client agrees to pay the Service Provider:

* **₹[Amount] per month** as managed service fees
* Additional charges for out-of-scope services, if applicable

Invoices shall be payable within **[30 days]** of receipt.

---

## 5. Client Responsibilities

The Client shall:

* Provide necessary system access and documentation
* Cooperate with the Service Provider during service delivery
* Maintain secure working environments

---

## 6. Confidentiality

Both Parties shall maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary, technical, and business information exchanged under this Agreement.

Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination.

---

## 7. Data Protection & Security

The Service Provider shall:

* Implement reasonable security safeguards
* Protect Client data from unauthorized access
* Notify the Client promptly of any data breach
* Comply with applicable Indian data protection laws

---

## 8. Intellectual Property Rights

Unless otherwise agreed:

* All deliverables developed for the Client shall become the Client’s property upon full payment
* Pre-existing intellectual property of the Service Provider shall remain its property

---

## 9. Independent Contractor Relationship

The Service Provider shall act as an independent contractor and not as an employee, agent, or partner of the Client.

---

## 10. Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

Total liability shall not exceed the total service fees paid in the preceding **12 months**.

---

## 11. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **60 days’ written notice**.

Immediate termination may occur in case of:

* Material breach
* Persistent service failure
* Data security violations

---

## 12. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control.

---

## 13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of **India**.

Any disputes shall be resolved through arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Venue: **[City, India]**.

---

## 14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes prior agreements.
