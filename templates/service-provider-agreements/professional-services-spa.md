## 1. Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to provide professional services including but not limited to:

* Consulting and advisory services
* Technical or professional support
* Project execution assistance
* Preparation of reports and documentation

## 2. Term of Agreement

This Agreement shall commence on **[Start Date]** and shall remain valid until **[End Date / Completion of Services]**, unless terminated earlier as per this Agreement.

## 3. Service Obligations

* Perform services with reasonable professional skill and care
* Meet agreed deadlines and deliverables
* Use qualified personnel for service execution
* Provide regular progress updates to the Client

---

* **₹[Amount]** as professional service fees

Both Parties shall maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary, financial, technical, and business information exchanged during this Agreement.

Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination.

---

Unless otherwise agreed:

* All deliverables prepared for the Client shall become the property of the Client upon full payment
* Pre-existing intellectual property of the Service Provider shall remain its property

---

## 7. Independent Contractor Relationship

The Service Provider shall act as an independent contractor and not as an employee, agent, or partner of the Client.

---

## 8. Warranties

The Service Provider warrants that:

* Services shall be performed professionally and diligently
* Deliverables shall not infringe third-party intellectual property rights

---

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

Total liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement.

---

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **30 days’ written notice**.

Immediate termination may occur in case of:

* Material breach
* Non-performance
* Misconduct

---

## 11. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays due to events beyond reasonable control.
