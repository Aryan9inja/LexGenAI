**[Marketing Agency / Service Provider Name]**, having its principal place of business at **[Service Provider Address]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Service Provider"**, which shall include its successors and permitted assigns)

## 1. Scope of Services

The Service Provider agrees to provide marketing services including but not limited to:

* Digital marketing and social media management
* Branding and promotional campaigns
* Content creation and advertising support
* Market research and analytics
* Campaign strategy planning and execution

Detailed deliverables and timelines shall be specified in **Annexure A / Statement of Work (SOW)**.

## 2. Term of Agreement

This Agreement shall commence on **[Start Date]** and shall remain valid until **[End Date / Completion of Campaigns]**, unless terminated earlier as per this Agreement.

## 3. Service Provider Obligations

The Service Provider shall:

* Perform services with reasonable professional skill and diligence
* Follow agreed campaign strategies and timelines
* Provide periodic reports and performance analytics
* Use lawful marketing practices compliant with applicable laws

---

## 4. Fees & Payment Terms

The Client agrees to pay the Service Provider:

* **₹[Amount]** as marketing service fees

All marketing content and campaigns shall require prior approval from the Client before public release.

---

Both Parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary business, customer, and marketing information exchanged during this Agreement.

Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property Rights

Unless otherwise agreed:

* All marketing materials created for the Client shall become the property of the Client upon full payment
* Pre-existing creative assets and tools of the Service Provider shall remain its property

---

## 8. Compliance with Laws

The Service Provider shall ensure that all marketing activities comply with applicable Indian laws, including advertising standards and data protection regulations.

---

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

Total liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement.

---

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **30 days’ written notice**.

Immediate termination may occur in case of:

* Material breach
* Non-performance
* Violation of laws or regulations

---

## 11. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control.
