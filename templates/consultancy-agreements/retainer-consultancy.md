AND

**[Consultant Name]**, residing at / having its principal place of business at **[Consultant Address]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Consultant"**, which shall include permitted assigns)

---

## 1. Engagement

The Client hereby retains the Consultant to provide ongoing advisory and consultancy services relating to:

**[Nature of Consultancy Services]**

The Consultant shall make themselves reasonably available to provide advice, recommendations, and support as required by the Client.

---

## 2. Term of Agreement

This Agreement shall commence on **[Start Date]** and remain valid for **[Duration – e.g., 12 months]**, unless terminated earlier as per this Agreement.

The Agreement may be renewed upon mutual written consent.

---

## 3. Scope of Services

The Consultant shall:

* Provide periodic advisory services
* Attend meetings as requested
* Provide professional recommendations and guidance
* Deliver reports where required

Specific tasks may be outlined in **Annexure A / Scope Schedule**.

---

## 4. Retainer Fees

The Client agrees to pay the Consultant:

* A monthly retainer fee of **₹[Amount]**

This fee shall cover agreed advisory services up to **[X hours per month]**.

Additional services beyond agreed hours shall be billed separately at **₹[Rate] per hour/day**.

---

## 5. Payment Terms

Invoices shall be submitted monthly and payable within **[15/30] days** of receipt.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

The Consultant shall maintain strict confidentiality of all Client information and shall not disclose any confidential information without prior written consent.

This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

All work products, reports, or materials created for the Client shall become the property of the Client upon full payment.

The Consultant retains ownership of pre-existing intellectual property.

---

## 8. Independent Contractor

The Consultant shall act as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, or agent of the Client.

---

## 9. Conflict of Interest

The Consultant shall disclose any potential conflicts of interest and shall not undertake work that directly competes with the Client’s interests without written approval.

---

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing **30 days’ written notice**.

Immediate termination may occur in case of:

* Material breach
* Misconduct
* Failure to perform obligations

---

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

Total liability shall not exceed the total retainer fees paid under this Agreement.

---

## 12. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays due to events beyond reasonable control.

---

## 13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of **India**.

Disputes shall be resolved through arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Venue: **[City, India]**.

---

## 14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes prior agreements.
