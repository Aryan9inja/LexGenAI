## 1. Purpose of Engagement

The Client hereby engages the Consultant to provide **strategic advisory and consulting services** relating to:

* Business growth strategy
* Market expansion planning
* Operational improvements
* Organizational development
* Long-term business planning

The Consultant shall:

* Provide strategic advice and recommendations
* Conduct business assessments and analysis
* Participate in strategic planning meetings
* Prepare reports, roadmaps, and strategic documents

## 5. Confidentiality

The Consultant shall maintain strict confidentiality of all business, financial, operational, and strategic information of the Client.

This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement.

---

All strategic plans, reports, and deliverables prepared under this Agreement shall become the property of the Client upon full payment.

---

The Consultant shall act as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, or agent of the Client.

---

## 8. Non-Conflict of Interest

The Consultant shall disclose any potential conflicts and shall not engage with direct competitors of the Client without prior written consent during the term of this Agreement.

---

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

Total liability shall not exceed the total consultancy fees paid under this Agreement.

---

* Material breach
* Misconduct
* Failure to perform obligations

---
